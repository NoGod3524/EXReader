3. Format details verified against Microsoft MS-PPT
   RT_Slide=0x03EE; RT_SlideAtom=0x03EF; RT_UserEditAtom=0x0FF5.
   0x0FD9 is HeadersFooters, not Slide. Atom recVer commonly equals zero;
   recVer=F identifies generic containers. Known document/slide/list types
   also accept the requested recVer=0 container variant.
   DocumentAtom.slideSize is at payload offset 0, in master units of
   1/576 inch (1/8 pt), not half-EMU. Default 5760 x 4320 is 720 x 540 pt.
   Record types:
   https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/openspecs/office_file_formats/ms-ppt/38fb1fa5-0a62-477a-8b14-178df22de812
   DocumentAtom:
   https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/openspecs/office_file_formats/ms-ppt/121f2728-3497-4a0a-829e-6f416fee2ee6

5. Known boundaries
   M1 exposes page count, slide geometry and optional SummaryInformation title,
   with blank drawing. It does not render text, shapes, images, animations,
   master inheritance, notes or embedded OLE objects. Masters and notes lists
   do not contribute pages; embedded storage streams are not selected as the
   top-level PowerPoint stream. Only the requested streams are extracted.
   Title supports UTF-16, Windows-1252 and UTF-8 property strings; unsupported
   encodings or malformed property payloads leave the title empty.
   Incremental snapshots use the last top-level DocumentContainer; full
   Current User/UserEdit/PersistDirectory edit-chain resolution is outside M1.
   Encrypted presentations are not supported. At most 10000 fixed pages.
